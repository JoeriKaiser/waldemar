About

My path into tech was not a straight line. I followed family tradition into catering school and spent years in hospitality, ending up behind the bar, where I learned adaptability and working under pressure. Coding was always the side passion: WordPress sites for acquaintances, Minecraft plugins, any excuse to boot up the PC.

When the pandemic hit, I made the switch for good. Udemy courses first, then a coding bootcamp, then a first internship as a web developer in 2022, and a full-time offer from the same company a year later. I have been building for the web ever since.
